================================================================================
EVENTCORE + AMBIENT (JSON под HellGateJson/EventCore, не HellGateSpawn_*.txt)
================================================================================

Имена врагов в EventCore / EventTrap — это те же ключи префабов, что и в списке
врагов выше (кеш EnemyPrefabRegistry / SpawnConfigExecutor). Ошибка в строке
= 0 заспавненных юнитов в логе.

--- Modal EventCore (dialogue + branches) ---

Full setup: docs/EventCore_Modal_Setup_Guide.md

Manifest: EventCore/eventcore_manifest.json → eventFiles[]
Spawn: X,Y,EnemyType|ec_event=eventcore_broker_gate,Count
Steps: EventCore/<Lang>/Stranger/eventcore_*_s*.json
Text pools: EventCore/<Lang>/Stranger/eventcore_lang.json

--- EventTrap (coordinate zone / suspicion / knockdown ambush) ---

Full setup: docs/EventTrap_Setup_Guide.md

Registry: EventCore/event_trap_registry.json
Anchors: EVENTTRAP,event_folder,x,y in HellGateSpawn_*.txt (logic only; no world trap).
World spikes: TRAP,key,x,y,count on separate lines (SpawnConfigExecutor); not EventTrap anchors.
Content: EventCore/_shared/<eventFolder>/config.json + EventCore/<Lang>/<eventFolder>/phrases.json
spawnEnemyType: enemy key from this file (e.g. Undead)
